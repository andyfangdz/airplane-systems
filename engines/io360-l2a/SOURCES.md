# IO-360-L2A in the Cessna 172S NAV III

- The C172S uses the 180 hp rating at 2,700 RPM and a 76-inch, two-blade McCauley 1A170E/JHA7660. Section 2 supplies the engine limits. Section 4 gives an approximate warm idle of 600 RPM, the 1,800 RPM magneto check and the 2,300–2,400 RPM static check with the mixture leaned for maximum RPM. Figure 5-8 supplies paired RPM, power, airspeed and fuel flow at standard temperature. Section 7 describes automatic alternate air, the turbine fuel-flow transducer, the vapor return, the exhaust and the oil cooler. [Cessna 172S NAV III POH 172SPHBUS-00](https://aerocadet.com/cbts/pdf/172S-POH.pdf)
- Section 2 gives bore, stroke, compression ratio, ignition timing, dry weight, overall dimensions and the alternate rating. Sea-level power at 20 and 28 inHg, full-throttle manifold pressure, and full-throttle power at 20,000 ft were read approximately from the RPM lines in Figure 3-25, including the higher-speed extension for the 180 hp rating. Figure 3-9 supplies the fuel chart. The model interpolates between the plotted lines and extrapolates fuel flow above their RPM range. [Lycoming Operator’s Manual 60297-12](https://www.lycoming.com/content/operator%27s-manual-O-360-HO-360-IO-360-AIO-360-HIO-360-TIO-360-60297-12)
- The illustrated parts catalog defines the crankshaft, cylinder and rocker assemblies, the updraft injection servo, and the two Slick 4371 impulse-coupled magnetos. The model uses the roller-tappet option. Both rockers share one shaft, four bushings support that shaft, and six screws fasten each 61247 rocker cover. See chapters 72–74. [Lycoming PC-IO-360-L2A, June 2025](https://www.lycoming.com/sites/default/files/file/2025-06/PC-IO-360-L2A_0.pdf)
- The two nose inlets feed a pressure-cooled upper compartment. The right rear baffle carries the oil cooler, and the muffler shroud supplies cabin heat. The NAV III installation shown includes the engine-driven vacuum pump for the standby attitude indicator (POH pp. 7-35–37, 7-65). The tubular mount, rubber isolators and cooling baffles follow the 172 Series maintenance manual, chapters 71, 75 and 79. [Cessna 172 Series Maintenance Manual](https://www.aeroelectric.com/Reference_Docs/Cessna/cessna-maintenance-manuals/Cessna_172R_1996on_MM_C172RMM.pdf)

The fixed-pitch propeller unloads with increasing advance ratio. Its static power coefficient (0.060) and unloading coefficient (0.505) were fitted to the static and cruise points. The displayed reference pitch, propeller inertia, alternate-air door threshold, intake heating and thermal response are illustrative. The RSA-5AD1 nozzle relationship uses the curve identified for that unit in IO-390-C IOM Figure C-2. The cylinder-pressure trace and the lengths of hoses, castings and fasteners are schematic. The airframe contours, mount stations and duct routing are schematic. The exploded positions keep parts inspectable while the mechanism turns.
