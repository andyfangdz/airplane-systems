# IO-360-M1A in the Diamond DA40 XLS

- The M1A has a 361 cu in displacement and a 180 hp rating at 2,700 RPM. Page 9 gives dimensions of the bore and stroke, compression ratio, firing order, ignition timing, dry weight and accessory drive ratios. Figure 2-1 gives the envelope. Page 18 gives the performance and economy cruise settings. RPM lines in Figure 3-2 supplied approximate sea-level power at 20 and 28 inHg, full-throttle manifold pressure, and power at 20,000 ft. Figure 3-1 supplied the fuel-flow lines. The model interpolates the power chart and approximates the plotted fuel-flow slopes. [Lycoming Operation and Installation Manual 60297-36](https://www.lycoming.com/sites/default/files/attachments/%2528L%2529IO-360-M1A%2520Oper%2520%2526%2520Install%2520Manual%252060297-36.pdf)
- AFM §2 gives installed oil and temperature limits. Section 4A gives the idle and magneto checks. Section 7 and AMM chapters 61, 71, 73, 74 and 79 describe the governor, alternate-air sleeve, fuel flow transducer upstream of the servo, SlickSTART and remote cooler. The XLS propeller supplement establishes the three-blade MTV-12-B/183-59b, its 1.83 m diameter, blade limits of 11° and 30° at 0.75 radius and 2,700 RPM maximum continuous. [Diamond DA40 AFM 6.01.01-E](https://www.acbelleile.com/_files/ugd/ec5b88_820a629dca6a472ea7fe29b64398c055.pdf) · [Diamond AFMS SA06-52, pp. 2–3](https://diamondaviators.net/forum/download/file.php?id=1173) · [DA40 XLS installation sources](../../aircraft/da40/SOURCES.md)
- The IO-360 column of the parts catalog defines the front governor drive, parallel-valve heads, common rocker shafts, six-screw covers, forward-facing injection servo and Slick magnetos. The model depicts the roller-tappet option and the crankshaft assembly in Figure 6. The M1A operating manual has a general description mentioning dynamic counterweights. Figure 6 lists none for this assembly. The four exhaust headers and shrouded collector follow the XLS Power Flow installation. [Lycoming PC-(L)IO-360-M1A, Rev. 1](https://www.lycoming.com/sites/default/files/file/2025-10/PC-%28L%29IO-360-M1A%20Rev%201.pdf) · [Power Flow PFS-15250-00, pp. 3–8](https://www.powerflowsystems.com/documents/installation-icaw-ipc-ac-manufacture/diamond-da40-360-icaw)
- The left cowling inlet feeds the upper and lower intake baffles. The right cowling duct seals to the frame-mounted oil cooler and supplies the heater and alternator branches. The baffle tie bars, riveted seals, shock mounts and five firewall attachments follow chapters 71-10, 71-20, 75-00 and 79-00. [Diamond DA40 Series AMM 6.02.01](https://www.riedel.aero/wp-content/uploads/other/60201-r7-DA40-AMM-complete.pdf)
