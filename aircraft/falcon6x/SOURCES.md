# Falcon 6X model references

[Compare four rendered views of the model](preview.jpg) with the photographs and drawings below.

## Airframe and landing gear

The exterior uses the front, side and plan views in Dassault’s _Overview Falcon 6X_, DGAC-DMF-5180G, June 2026, p. 7. The drawings establish the wing sweep and dihedral, engine and wheel positions, tail outline and relative proportions. Local cross-sections and fairing contours are estimates fitted to those views and photographs. The September 2023 _Facts & Figures_, p. 3, also gives a three-view with rounded dimensions. The EASA sheet, issued 3 September 2025, identifies the two aft-mounted PW812D engines and Safran SPU150[DA] auxiliary power unit on p. 9. Section III.22 on p. 12 gives radial tubeless tire sizes H33 × 10.5 R17 for the main wheels and single-chine 16 × 6.0 R6 for the nose wheels. The model uses their nominal tire diameters of 0.838 m and 0.406 m respectively. Dassault’s 2026 Overview, pp. 60–61, identifies the slats, flap, flaperon, aileron and airbrakes and describes the flaperon’s roles in lift and roll. ## Cabin and flight deck

- The representative layout follows the 14-passenger row of the 2026 Overview, p. 12, and Global Jet’s July 2025 operator sheet for LX-TIN, pp. 1–2. The forward club seats four, the port conference table seats four opposite a starboard credenza, and two facing aft divans seat three each. The operator’s photographs document the seat profiles, wood ledges and upholstery on LX-TIN. F-WSIX supplies the exterior paint reference. The certified limit is 19 passengers, subject to the individual aircraft’s approved seating plan (EASA.A.580, pp. 11–12). [Dassault cabin plans](https://www.marketingfalcon.com/brochures/6XOverview/SK90Q7rK7:MaL7e2oH) · [Global Jet LX-TIN sheet and photographs](https://globaljet.aero/sites/default/files/2025-07/Dassault%20Falcon%206X_LX-TIN_0.pdf) · [EASA capacity limits](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/downloads/138391/en#page=11)
- A 6X cockpit photograph published by 50SKYSHADES at EBACE on 29 May 2022 shows four main displays, the glareshield controls, outboard sidesticks, twin thrust levers and paired keyboards. Dassault describes the EASy IV avionics and cursor controls. The Overview, p. 57, illustrates the electronic flight bags and dual-HUD capability. The model depicts a left HUD and illustrative display pages. ## PW812D engine cutaway

EASA IM.E.096, issue 07, 8 October 2025, p. 7, specifies the two spools. The fan and two booster stages share a shaft with four low-pressure turbine stages. The high-pressure spool has eight compressor stages and two turbine stages, with an annular combustor between compressor and turbine. The nominal fan-case diameter is 1.109 m and the spinner-face-to-tail-cone length is 2.874 m. Page 8 gives 13,850 lbf for five-minute takeoff under the static test conditions in note 2, p. 11. Dassault quotes 13,500 lbf per engine at sea level in its 2026 Overview, p. 65. [Engine type certificate](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/downloads/33453/en#page=7) · [Dassault engine overview](https://www.marketingfalcon.com/brochures/6XOverview/SK90Q7rK7:MaL7e2oH)

The PW812D photographs on p. 65 and Pratt & Whitney’s PW800 family images inform the fan, combustor and exhaust mixer shapes. Dassault’s February 2024 aircraft backgrounder identifies the nacelle and thrust reverser. Blade counts within each row, blade profiles, internal spacing, combustor perforations and mixer contours are illustrative.
