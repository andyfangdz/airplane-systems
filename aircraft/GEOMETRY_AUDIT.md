## What the photos do and do not establish

- The subsequent [three-view trace audit](reference-traces/README.md) corrects the SR20 fin height, wing outlines and tail-tip shapes. Span, length, wheelbase, gear track and control travel are unchanged. The SR20 rudder and its attached details follow the corrected fin height.
- Local cross-section exponents, inlet outline sizes, propeller chord profiles, bracket dimensions and fairing contours are **visual approximations**. They are not measurements from calibrated orthographic photographs or engineering drawings. Perspective, camera height and in-flight attitude prevent treating pixel ratios as exact dimensions.
- Photo review alone did not justify replacing the fuselage station tables. The later calibrated handbook traces identify additional planform and fin-height corrections, documented with before/after overlays. Airfoil sections, window glazing and small fittings remain simplified study-model representations; neither audit certifies the models as exact.
- Solid-mode livery artwork and registrations are unchanged. Historical paint, equipment on other registrations, and photographic propeller position are not copied into the aircraft's system configuration.

## Verification

Use `npm run shot -- sr20/overview c172s/overview c182t/overview da40/overview --solid --no-labels` for the standard views. During the audit, the four models were also inspected from consistent side, front, top and front-quarter cameras, before and after, and switched back to x-ray. Check the engine, gear and controls views as well as the overview; the DA40 canopy still needs to open independently of its aft door.

`tests/exterior-contours.test.ts` checks that local cross-section changes remain consistent across skin projection, point containment and ring generation; inlet meshes sit outside the nose and face forward; and composite fairings leave the tire's lower arc exposed. These are geometry-consistency checks, not claims that photo-fitted dimensions are exact.

Cowling inlets are open geometry in both solid and x-ray modes. The SR20 and Cessna nose skins have matching cutouts behind their rims; their former opaque inlet disks are short, uncapped inner walls. The Cessna lower induction openings also expose the existing filters behind them. The DA40's round nose openings are uncapped, and its lower inlet is cut through the skin as well. The subtraction is limited to the forward cowling and preserves interpolated paint UVs and normals. Throat depth is a visual approximation, not a measured internal duct layout. Ray tests check the openings, surrounding skin and protection of the aft skin.

## M20C gear doors and entry step follow-up

The [Mooney M20 Series Service & Maintenance Manual](https://mxdocs.thrustinstitute.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/M20_Series_MM.pdf), §§5-2, 5-11 and 5-12 (printed 5-1, 5-8 and 5-9; PDF 53, 60 and 61), describes main doors linked to the legs, and paired nose doors operated by link rods (Fig. 5-2, printed 5-2). The extra inboard main doors in Fig. 5-13 are explicitly **M20J-only**. Preserve the existing folding main assemblies, wheel wells and separately hinged nose doors. Shorten the lower edge of the schematic main-door panel while retaining its upper edge, attachment and gear animation.

The [close-up photograph of an M20F main leg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rubber_trailing_link_main_landing_gear.JPG) supports the shared arrangement: the door sits beside the leg and wheel, with the lower tyre exposed. It is a qualitative reference from another variant, not a measurement of N6947N. Low door clearance is characteristic; neither the photograph nor the cited manual establishes an exact ground-clearance dimension for this model. The previous rectangular panel came within 15 mm of the modeled ground; shortening its lower edge gives 135 mm in the gear-down pose. Those are **model coordinates, not measured aircraft specifications**. The main doors remain simplified panels carried with the legs; the exact outline, separate linkage and wheel-well closure are not reproduced. Tests verify ground and tyre clearance throughout the existing animation.

The [Mooney model chronology](https://www.mooneyevents.com/chrono.htm) identifies the **1968 M20C Ranger, s/n 680001–680198**, as having a fixed entrance step. Retain that configuration for the modeled s/n 680194. The earlier vacuum-retracted step is not appropriate here. The old support ended outside the rounded fuselage; its replacement starts inside the actual skin and meets the tread. Both pieces belong to the airframe and cabin views. The tube contour is approximate.

`tests/m20c-exterior.test.ts` checks main-door ground and tyre clearance through the gear cycle and physical step attachment. Existing `tests/m20c-placement.test.ts` checks gear, well and nose-door placement. Inspect solid and x-ray gear views with the gear down and up, plus the step from the right side and below; use `npm run shot -- m20c/gear m20c/airframe m20c/cabin --solid --no-labels` for the standard views.
