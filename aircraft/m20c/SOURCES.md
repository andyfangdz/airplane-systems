# 1968 M20C Ranger study configuration

Research inspected 12 October 2026 for the model identified as N6947N, serial 680194.
This is a systems study model, not an installation drawing, flight checklist or maintenance instruction.
The existing registration/photograph identification is retained; aircraft equipment lists, STC records and logs
were not supplied. Component sizes, mounting coordinates, swept clearances and route locations are illustrative.

## Disagreements resolved and model limits

- The baseline asserted all 1968 Rangers used an alternator. Actual POH1187 permits both charging sources. The study
  preserves its existing alternator logic and browser behavior, but now names it a selected option and leaves N6947N's
  installed source unverified. Generator-specific regulator/load-meter behavior is not used in the alternator solver.
- The baseline used **8.7:1** and fuel **91/98**. Actual 1968 scan p. 9 specifies **8.5:1**; p. 10 specifies **91/96** minimum.
  These source values are corrected. Modern approved fuels/replacement parts require current engine/aircraft documents.
- The 1968 manual distinguishes the **steady stall horn** from the **intermittent, lower-pitched gear horn**. Later
  combined vacuum annunciation behavior is not substituted for this aircraft's separate HI/LO lights.
- A commonly indexed [M20C Ranger PDF](https://www.mattbeyer.com/poh/Mooney-M20C-Ranger.pdf) is **December 1974,
  serial 20-1147 and later**, as its title page states. It was inspected but is not mislabelled as the 1968 book.
  Electric gear, revised panel layouts and later warning-light descriptions from it do not override 1968 configuration.
- Optional full-flow filter, alternate static, heated pitot and radio cases in the original model are schematic
  study items with unverified installation details. Filter and original pressure-screen depictions are comparative
  views of filtration configurations, not proof that both are installed together.
- Manual gear and wheel/door datums, Johnson-bar lock/latch interaction, stroke-pumped flaps, propeller behavior,
  whole-tail articulation and the original browser regression flow are preserved. Fault responses (screen restriction,
  flap leakage, PC leak, high vacuum, magnetos, cooler obstruction, heater leak and protective alternator trip) are
  qualitative; no measured leak rate, CO concentration, regulator calibration or failure life is implied.
- PC command particles show the opposing suction/atmospheric-vent outputs in Brittain 11968 § 2.2; neutral demand
  has no differential movement. The display follows the model's qualitative roll/yaw demands, not separately
  controlled physical gyro axes or calibrated airflow. Primary vacuum at the pneumatic relay remains independent
  of cut-off or the study servo-line-leak fault; the relay's sequenced feed and command outputs stop when PC is disabled.
- The wing's schematic buried root was moved inward to connect its physical skin to the narrowed curved lower
  fuselage. Tip/span and dimensioned wheel track/wheelbase remain unchanged. The equivalent fuel display envelope
  was recalibrated to **26 US gal per wing**; it is not a surveyed tank volume drawing.
- Internal hardware and routes are checked against the model's actual curved fuselage/wing/tail skin union, including
  tube radius and neutral parent transforms. Only narrow, named physical vents/drain/probe throats receive aperture
  allowances. Genuine exterior gear hoses, exposed horns and propeller components retain exterior metadata.
  New wheel hardware/hose motion is checked throughout gear travel; existing placement tests cover full control/tail/flap motion.
- Wing tip, leading edge and side-specific cowling highlights are narrow, skin-conforming inspection regions,
  visible only when spotlighted. Their bounds/offsets are display choices. The inspected 1968 sources did not
  establish an SR22T-style stall-strip installation, so none is added by assumption.

## Cabin routing verification

Cabin wiring, fuel/pressure lines and PC tubes follow side-trim/underfloor lanes, using a clear forward post where the narrow lower cabin cannot accommodate a line beside a cushion. The main-output breaker remains on the source-supported 1968 right upholstery panel, with its schematic lateral offset moved clear of the copilot seat back. Circuit and manual-system behavior is unchanged. Complete tube vertices, triangle centers and edge midpoints are checked against occupied cabin space and actual seat meshes, alongside the existing skin-containment checks.
